# Portfolio writing

Write as if answering a developer who asks what you built.

- Start with what it does or what you found. Use concrete verbs.
- Keep claims inside the evidence. Pair numbers with their test scope and comparison; label prototypes and unfinished work.
- Prefer a specific detail over broad claims about AI or personal values.
- Use first person when it adds context. Keep the name in the site branding; do not repeat it in body copy.
- Draft English and Simplified Chinese independently. Keep each concise and natural in its language.
